# 🍃 Hayao Miyazaki (宮崎 駿) — Studio Ghibli Retrospective

> *"The future is not a straight line. There are many different pathways to another kind of future, where heart and nature can connect."*  
> — **Hayao Miyazaki**

A cinematic retrospective and digital tribute honoring **Hayao Miyazaki (宮崎 駿)** — visionary co-founder of Studio Ghibli, master animator, screenwriter, and pioneer of hand-crafted world cinema.

---

## 🌸 Highlights & Features

- **🎨 Studio Ghibli Aesthetic:** Floating watercolor clouds backdrop, soot-sprite badges, and Japanese calligraphy (*宮崎 駿*).
- **🎬 Landmark Cinematic Masterworks (Color-Coded Cards):**
  - **1988 — My Neighbor Totoro (*となりのトトロ*):** Pastoral poem of childhood and forest spirits.
  - **1997 — Princess Mononoke (*もののけ姫*):** Epic conflict between human industry and primordial nature gods.
  - **2001 — Spirited Away (*千と千尋の神隠し*):** Mythological bathhouse masterpiece; 2003 Academy Award Winner.
  - **2023 — The Boy and the Heron (*君たちはどう生きるか*):** Introspective meditation; 2024 Academy Award Winner.
- **📜 The Ghibli Journey & Artistic Ethos:** Structured biography exploring his fascination with wartime flight mechanics, founding of Studio Ghibli in 1985, musical partnership with Joe Hisaishi, and dedication to hand-drawn cel animation.
- **🏆 Cultural Honors & Legacy:** Tribute to his historic two Academy Awards for Best Animated Feature, Academy Honorary Award (2014), and global cinematic legacy.
- **📱 Fully Responsive:** Adaptive layout on mobile, tablet, and widescreen devices.

---

## 🛠️ Built With
